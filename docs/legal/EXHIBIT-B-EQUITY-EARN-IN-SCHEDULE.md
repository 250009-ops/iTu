# EXHIBIT B — EQUITY AND PROFIT AWARD TABLE

**Attached to:** Co-Founder Operating Agreement  
**Parties:** Sarvarbek Erkinov and Ozodbek To'lqinov

---

## 1. Ownership Start

- Founder side starts at **100% equity**.
- Co-founder side starts at **0% equity**.
- Co-founder maximum equity is **20%**.

---

## 2. First Milestone (Fixed)

First milestone threshold is fixed at **$50 cumulative net profit**.

When first milestone is passed:
- Co-founder gets **+5% equity**,
- Co-founder profit share increases from **25% to 30%**,
- Founder share adjusts accordingly.

---

## 3. Future Awards (Visible, Thresholds Later)

Future milestone thresholds are set after first milestone review, in writing.

| Award Stage | Equity Add | Co-Founder Equity Total | Co-Founder Profit Share |
|-------------|------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| First milestone ($50 net profit) | +5% | 5% | 30% |
| Next award stage (threshold set later) | +5% | 10% | 35% |
| Next award stage (threshold set later) | +5% | 15% | 40% |
| Final award stage (threshold set later) | +5% | 20% max | 45% max |

> **NOTICE — After 20% Equity Is Reached**  
> At **20% equity**, co-founder profit share is already at maximum (**45%**).  
> After this stage, both parties must run a formal decision process to decide whether the equity limit should be increased through a new milestone cycle.  
> Any increase to the equity limit requires written and signed approval. Profit share remains capped at **45%**.

---

## 4. Grant Conditions

Every award release requires:
1. milestone confirmation in writing,
2. founder-side approval,
3. formal award record.

---

## 5. Freeze and Penalty Link

If co-founder is under enforcement suspension:
- no new equity award is released,
- no profit-share increase is released,
- deferred/reduced variable benefit may apply.

---

## 6. Vesting

Each released equity award follows:
- **4-year vesting**,
- **1-year cliff**,
- monthly vesting after cliff.

Unvested equity is forfeited if agreement ends before vesting completion.
