Responsibility and Control

  • Founder side handles technical stack, architecture, infrastructure, security, and technical-risk approvals.
  • Co-founder side controls full operations/admin implementation.
  • Founder side keeps veto only on technical-risk matters.

Admin Capabilities Manager Must Handle

  • Pricing and package control
  • Sales and operations execution
  • Process and policy implementation
  • Business model improvements including subscriptions
  • Milestone execution and reporting

Profit and Equity

  • Initial net-profit split: Founder 75%, Co-founder 25%.
  • First milestone is fixed at $50 cumulative net profit.
  • At first milestone: equity becomes 5%, and co-founder share becomes 30% (25% + 5%).
  • Max co-founder outcomes: 20% equity and 45% net-profit share.

NOTICE — 20% Equity Cap and Next Decision Process
  • When co-founder equity reaches 20% (maximum under this agreement), co-founder profit share is already at its maximum stage of 45%.
  • At that stage, both parties enter a formal decision-making process to review whether the equity limit should be increased through the next milestone cycle.
  • Any new equity limit must be set in writing and signed by both parties.
  • Profit share does not increase beyond 45%, even if the equity limit is raised later.

Sequence: product price -> tax -> total -> platform fee -> net amount.

Penalty Rule
