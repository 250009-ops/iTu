# EXHIBIT A — KPI, ADMIN CONTROL, AND PENALTY RULES

**Attached to:** Co-Founder Operating Agreement  
**Parties:** Sarvarbek Erkinov and Ozodbek To'lqinov

---

## 1. Purpose

This exhibit defines how Party B operational performance is measured and how penalties apply if expected outcomes are not achieved.

---

## 2. Party B Performance Scope

Party B is evaluated on:
- operations implementation quality,
- growth execution and conversion outcomes,
- delivery speed and accountability,
- stability of administrative processes,
- reporting discipline and transparency.

---

## 3. Reporting Duties

Party B must provide:
- regular weekly operations and growth updates,
- monthly summary with achieved vs expected outcomes,
- corrective plan when performance is below expectation.

---

## 4. Suspend-First Penalty Model

If expected outcomes are not met:

1. Co-founder variable release can be suspended.
2. Equity progression can be frozen.
3. Party B must execute a written recovery plan.
4. Party A may reduce or defer co-founder variable benefit for repeated underperformance.
5. If failure persists after correction period, Party A may terminate the agreement.

---

## 5. Documentation Rule

All warnings, freezes, penalties, and recovery decisions must be recorded in writing.
