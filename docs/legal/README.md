## Business Logic Captured

- Founder side: tech stack and technical-risk control.
- Co-founder side: full operations/admin implementation control.
- Base net-profit split: **75% Founder / 25% Co-founder**.
- First milestone fixed: **$50 cumulative net profit**.
- At first milestone:
  - equity rises to **5%**,
  - co-founder net-profit share becomes **30%**.
- Max co-founder outcomes:
  - **20% equity**,
  - **45% net-profit share**.

## Bordered Notice Rules

- **20% equity cap review:** At 20% equity, profit share is already at max (45%). Both parties then run a formal decision process to review whether the equity limit should be increased through the next milestone cycle. Any increase requires written signed approval. Profit share stays capped at 45%.
- **Tech fee notification:** CTO Founder (Party A) must notify CEO Co-founder (Party B) in writing whenever technical fees or new technical costs arise. CEO must apply necessary financial considerations in pricing, budgeting, and profit planning.
