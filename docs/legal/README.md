## Business Logic Captured

- Operating agreement — not a financial services contract.
- Bosh Director: tech stack and technical-risk control.
- CEO: full operations/admin implementation control.
- Base net-profit split: **75% Bosh Director / 25% CEO**.
- First milestone fixed: **$50 cumulative net profit**.
- At first milestone:
  - equity rises to **5%**,
  - CEO net-profit share becomes **30%**.
- Max CEO outcomes:
  - **20% equity**,
  - **45% net-profit share**.

## Confidentiality Rules

- Source code, architecture, customer data, business strategy, and credentials are confidential.
- No third-party disclosure without written approval from the Bosh Director.
- Obligation survives termination; breach is a material violation.

## Bordered Notice Rules

- **20% equity cap review:** At 20% equity, profit share is already at max (45%). Both parties then run a formal decision process to review whether the equity limit should be increased through the next milestone cycle. Any increase requires written signed approval. Profit share stays capped at 45%.
